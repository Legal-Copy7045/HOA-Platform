# Glossary: one name for each thing

The portal uses one name for each thing, the same in the menu, the page title
(`<h1>`), dashboard tiles, emails and letters. Owners see plain words; the code
may use shorter or older names (right-hand column). When you add a page, use the
name here, and make its menu label match its `<h1>` (a test checks this).

| What owners and the board see | Meaning | Name in code |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | The home page after sign-in: to-dos first, then numbers. | `home`, `core/dashboard.py` |
| Home, address | A property in the community, always named by its street address, e.g. 165 Imaginary Orchard Way. The plat lot number is not used as a name (D40): it shows only as a small line under a home's title, in brackets on formal documents ("165 Imaginary Orchard Way (Lot 30)": resale certificate, statements and delinquency letters, ARC decision letters, violation notices, the printed neighbor acknowledgement form), and as a last "Lot number" column in the roster, accounting and finance exports. The board can still search and filter by it. | `Lot` (`Lot.label`, `Lot.legal_label`) |
| Register import / Import homes and owners | The board loads the list of homes (lot number and address) and their owners from a CSV or Excel file: download the template, upload, check what would change (nothing written yet), then import. Homes that already have owners are left alone; importing the same file again adds nothing. The file is scanned for malware, kept privately for the board and deleted after the import or after 30 days (D54). | `register_import`, `community/register_import.py`, `RegisterImport` |
| Home account | The board's page for one home's money: entries, payments, charges and letters. | `lot_ledger` |
| Home neighbors | Each home's left and right neighbors from the development plan, and other homes a project there affects. | `arc_neighbors` |
| Menu sections | **My home** (your own home, account and requests), **Community**, **Association** (the board's work), **Maintenance** (work orders, vendors, contracts), **Finance** and **Admin**. The last four sit under one **Board** heading (D64); a former owner's one section is **My history**. Ending a board seat removes only those. | `core/navigation.py` `SECTIONS` |
| Needs your attention / My items | Everything waiting on you. The dashboard list, the My items page and the menu bubbles all come from one list. Set-up warnings (fewer than two people able to act, board terms past their end, rules missing a citation) sit at the top of My items, then one line of links to its groups (each named after its menu entry: "Owner requests"). The shortcut "Record payments received" opens Record payments (owners' payments coming in); "Write checks" is for checks the association writes. | `core.my_items.items_for` |
| Can't sign in? | The one help link on the sign-in page (D62): a short page that asks what's wrong (forgot password; lost phone, passkey or app; setup link expired or never finished; no access to email; locked out) and opens the right next step. "Ask the board" there asks for a new setup link; the board sees "<person> asked for a new setup link" on My items. It never says whether an account exists. | `signin_help`, `accounts/signin_help.py`, `accounts/setup_requests.py` |
| Account recovery | Recovering your sign-in yourself after losing your phone or passkey and your recovery codes (homeowners, account contacts and tenants only): a waiting period (48 hours by default) with alerts to every address on file and the board, then a new passkey or app, then a lock on votes, proxies, contact details and automatic payments for a while. Board members and staff use the two-board-member reset instead. The person's page is **Account recovery**; the board's page for one request has the same name. | `accounts/recovery.py`, `AccountRecovery`, `account_recovery_status`, `recovery_board` |
| Second approvals | Requests a second board member must approve (board seats, sysadmin nominations, sign-in resets, account changes). Reached from My items, where these to-dos are grouped as "Second approvals" (account changes under "Homes and owners"). | `approvals` |
| Preview as owner | A board member or manager sees the portal as one home's owner does, read-only and logged. Started from the home's page. | `core/preview.py` |
| Search | The search box at the top of the menu and its results page: finds announcements, documents, meetings, requests and more, grouped by area. Shows only what you can already open. | `core/search.py`, `<app>/search.py` |
| My home | Your home (its address as the title, the plat lot number small beneath), the people on your account. | `my_lot` |
| Assessment | The regular monthly charge, named as the covenants name it: a Community settings value ("What the regular monthly charge is called", with its citation; default Assessment, the Act's word). Pages, emails, letters and new charges use it; the code still says "dues". | `charge.term`, `community/terms.py`, `{% term %}` |
| Monthly statement email | Sent when the month's assessment is posted, to owners and authorized or view-only contacts who leave it ticked on My profile: amount due, what to do, links, the statement PDF. | `ledger/statement_email.py`, `StatementEmail` |
| My account | Your statement and balance. The board's view of every home is **Owner accounts**; one home's page is its **Home account**. | `ledger` |
| Open charges | Each charge on an account (assessments, late fees, other charges, resale fees) with what is still unpaid on it and the payments applied to it. | `ledger.allocation`, `PaymentApplication` |
| Invoice 2026-0142 / Assessment – March 2026 | A charge's number (year and sequence) and its name. | `ChargeNumber` |
| Pay online | An owner or authorized contact pays chosen charges on Stripe's hosted page: bank account or debit card (no fee), or credit card (with the surcharge). | `pay_online`, `payments.checkout` |
| Payment status | The page Stripe returns the payer to; the portal asks Stripe itself whether the payment went through. | `pay_return` |
| Credit card surcharge | The fee on credit card payments only (never bank or debit), at most 3%. Not part of the account balance. | `payments.credit_card_surcharge_bp` |
| Pay assessments automatically | Automatic payments: each monthly assessment (never late fees or other charges) is paid on its due day, or the next 12 months' assessments once a year in a month the payer chooses, from a bank account (no fee) or card (credit cards with the surcharge) the payer saved on Stripe's page. Turned off any time on My account; ends when the home is sold. | `autopay`, `payments.autopay` |
| Once a year (automatic payments) | The annual schedule: one payment for the next 12 months' assessments less any credit, which then pays each month's assessment as it is charged (prepaid credit). | annual autopay, `AutopaySchedule.ANNUAL` |
| Waiting for the board's review | An automatic payment above the review limit ($500 by default) that is not charged until a board member who does not pay for that home approves it; declined or not decided in 14 days, nothing is charged. | `AutopayReview`, `payments.autopay_review_cents` |
| Online payments | The board's page of Stripe payments, bank payments processing, problems to handle, payouts and the daily check against Stripe. | `online_payments`, `payments.sync` |
| Disbursements | The board's page of money paid back to owners: surplus distributions and owner refunds, each proposed, approved by other board members, then paid by statement credit or check. Finance menu. | `disbursements`, `ledger/disbursements.py` |
| Disbursement | One proposal to pay money back: its homes, amounts and how each is paid; approved or declined by board members other than the one who proposed it. | `DisbursementBatch`, `DisbursementLine` |
| Surplus distribution | A fiscal year's surplus credited back under a board resolution (68 Pa.C.S. § 5313; the Declaration's surplus section): in proportion to the assessments paid (or equally per home), credited to the home's account (or to the owner who paid), by statement credit, or by check only where the board allows it. | `disbursement.surplus_by_share_paid`, `disbursement.surplus_stays_with_home`, `disbursement.surplus_cash_allowed` |
| Owner refund | One account's credit paid back by check, usually to a former owner after a sale. Never more than the account's credit. | `DisbursementKind.REFUND` |
| Statement credit | A credit on an owner's account that pays the next charges ("Surplus distribution credit – 2026"). | `credit` entry |
| Check register | The list of checks a disbursement needs (payee, mailing address, amount, memo, check number), as CSV or PDF, for writing the checks in the bank's bill pay or the accounting package. | `disbursement_register` |
| Check printing | Where each field prints on the association's pre-printed check stock, with an alignment test page. | `check_print_settings` |
| Account number | The number of one ownership period of a home (changes when the home is sold). Shown as plain text, not a badge. | `Account.number` |
| My home improvement requests (ARC) | Asking the Architectural Review Committee to approve an exterior change, and neighbors' projects you are asked to acknowledge. The board's list is **Architectural review**. | `arc_list?mine=1`, `ArcRequest` |
| Neighbor acknowledgement | A neighbor confirms they have seen a project. It never decides the request. | "signoff" (`arc/signoffs.py`) |
| Notices about my home | Covenant or rule notices sent to an owner. The board's list is **Violations**. | `compliance`, `Violation` |
| Fix by | The date an owner should fix a notice by. | `cure_by` |
| Official notices | The formal notices the association must send every owner on the deed (meeting, budget and assessment notices, delinquency letters, others the Declaration or law requires), by email to those who agreed, by post to everyone else. Not the same as **Notices about my home**. | `notice_choice`, `community/notices.py` |
| Paper mail | The board's and manager's page for official notices by post: who gets paper (owners on the deed who have not agreed to email), new mailings, printing letters, envelopes and labels, and marking them mailed. **Paper mail settings** (on Settings) holds where the address prints for window envelopes and the USPS tracking barcode. | `paper_mail`, `comms/papermail.py` |
| Mailing | One paper mailing: its wording, who it goes to (one letter per home and mailing address), when it was mailed. Kept permanently. | `comms.Mailing` |
| USPS tracking | Reading USPS Informed Visibility scans of barcoded letters (optional feature). A letter is Mailed, Processed, Sorted for delivery on a date (usually delivered that day or the next), or flagged with No scans after 5 days. | `usps_tracking`, `comms/usps_tracking.py`, `comms/usps_iv.py` |
| Sorted for delivery | USPS sorted the letter into the carrier's route (operation 918/919): the last scan USPS gives without an eDoc. | `SORTED_OPCODES` |
| How we reach you | The card on My profile with the official notices choice, emails, phones (and opt-in text alerts) and the one mailing address for post. | `my_profile` |
| Mailing address for post | Where paper notices and letters go: the home's address or one address the person enters. "Address on file (please check)" is one typed before the separate fields existed. | `ContactProfile.mail_*`, `postal_address` |
| Text alerts | Optional texts about urgent community matters, only after ticking the box and entering a texted code. | `comms/sms.py` |
| Approved automatically | No decision was made within the review period, so the Declaration treats the request as approved. | "deemed approval" |
| Can't take part in the review | A reviewer who is the applicant, owns or is a contact of the home or an affected home, or owned the home when the request was made (D65). | "recused" (`is_recused`) |
| Checked for completeness | The board checks a submitted request has everything it needs, then the review clock starts. | `submitted` state |
| Request #12, Notice #7, Work order #3 | Record numbers. No letter prefixes (ARC-12, V-7, WO-3, R-2). | primary key |
| Version 2 | A document's second upload. | `DocumentVersion.number` |
| My requests | Questions and requests to the board. The board's list is **Owner requests**. View-only contacts see the requests on the account they follow, read-only; former owners find theirs in **My history**. | `operations.OwnerRequest` |
| Records requests | An owner's request to inspect association records (Pennsylvania law). The owner's own list is **My records requests**. | `governance.RecordsRequest` |
| Voting | One list of **Votes**, **Elections** and **Surveys**, filtered by open or past and by type. Board members start new ones here (New vote, New election, New survey). Owners also find **Give a proxy** for coming owners' meetings. The old addresses of the three lists lead here. | `voting` |
| Votes | Formal votes of the owners: binding, one ballot per home, with the quorum and pass rules (and citations) fixed when the vote opens. Informal polls made before November 26, 2026 stay readable under Past; a new poll is a survey with one question. | `Vote` |
| Elections | Board elections: nominations, ballots, results. | `Election` |
| Surveys | Informal multi-question surveys from the board (not a vote). Anonymous ones keep answers apart from who responded. Someone who can only answer surveys (a tenant) sees the Voting list as **Surveys**, in the menu and the heading. | `Survey` |
| Survey results | Totals for each question once the survey has closed (nobody sees them while it is open, D61); written answers are seen by the board only. Owners see totals after it closes, if the board shares them. | `survey_results` |
| Voting window | When a formal vote or an election opens and when it closes, both set when it opens (D61). The closing time is at least the shortest voting period after the opening (Community settings, default 7 days). Voting closes by itself at the deadline ("Vote by Dec 3, 2026 5:00 PM"); the board can extend it once, with a reason, before it passes, never shorten it, and closes early only if Community settings allow it. Owners who have not voted get a reminder 48 hours before. | `governance/window.py`, `vote.min_open_days` |
| Turnout | While voting is open, how many homes have voted out of all homes ("12 of 44 homes have voted (27%)"), the quorum needed and whether it is reached so far; per person for surveys. The board and the manager also see **Who has voted** (addresses only, never choices). Nobody sees how the votes are going until voting closes. Owners see the turnout only if Community settings allow it. | `window.turnout`, `vote.turnout`, `vote.show_turnout_to_owners` |
| Proxy | Someone you choose to vote for your home at a meeting. Holders are picked by name (board members) or by address (owners). | `Proxy` |
| Resolutions and policies | Board resolutions and the policies in force. The board also adopts a resolution by written consent here and sees those **Waiting for signatures**. | `Resolution` |
| Adopt by written consent | A board decision without a meeting: every serving board member must approve in writing; one decline and it is not adopted. The signatures are kept as their own records; once adopted it is recorded as a resolution. | `ConsentRequest`, `consents` (redirects to Resolutions and policies) |
| Calendar | Meetings and community events in one list, filtered by **All**, **Meetings**, **Events** and **Past meetings** (with minutes once approved). The board starts new ones here (**New event**, **New meeting**). Executive sessions show only in the board's view. With the calendar switched off, **Meetings** is its own menu entry again. The old Meetings address leads to the Meetings filter. | `calendar?type=...`, `meetings` (redirects) |
| Meeting page | Everything about one meeting: where (place, link to join online, or both), agenda, notice, minutes (uploaded here), packet, the votes, elections and resolutions that belong to it, quorum and proxies. The board's steps sit in **Board actions**, in order. Meetings put themselves on the calendar and come off it when cancelled; executive sessions never appear on the owners' calendar. | `meeting_detail` |
| Join link | The link to join an online or hybrid meeting or event. Shown only on the portal to people who can open the meeting, never in emails. | `Meeting.join_url`, `Event.join_url` |
| Add to calendar | Puts an event or meeting in your own calendar: Google Calendar, Outlook.com, Microsoft 365, or a calendar file (.ics) for Apple Calendar, Outlook on a computer and others. The file needs sign-in; there is no public feed. | `comms/calendar_links.py`, `event_ics`, `meeting_ics` |
| Meeting packet | One PDF for a meeting: cover, agenda, minutes, financial totals and an attachment index, with a .zip of the attachments. The **Board packet** (marked "Confidential – board only") adds receivables and open-item counts; the **Owner packet** never has delinquency, violation or collection details. | `governance/packets.py`, `MeetingPacket` |
| Community info | One page for **Frequently asked questions**, **Board and committees** and the **Owner directory**. Committees open from Board and committees (WT-008). With only one of these open to you, the menu goes straight to it. | `community_info` |
| Reserve plan | The board's in-house reserve study: the things the association will one day replace (component, cost today, useful life, years left), a year-by-year projection of the reserve fund, percent funded and the contribution needed. Finance menu. | `reserve_plan`, `ledger/reserves.py` |
| Record as adopted | The board fixes the current reserve plan's figures as adopted on a date ("reserve plan adopted Nov 26, 2026"); kept unchanged. | `ReserveStudySnapshot` |
| Budget and reserves | What owners see of the money plan: the budget (income, expenses, the monthly assessment per home) and the adopted reserve plan's summary (percent funded, adopted date, next major items; no board notes). The year is in the subtitle. The old Reserve plan summary page redirects here. | `owner_budget` (`reserve_summary` redirects) |
| Percent funded | The reserve balance compared with the fully funded balance (each component's cost times the share of its life already used). | `percent_funded` |
| Community setup | The board's step-by-step walk through every set-up decision (community details, the monthly charge, late fees, payments, notices, reviews, features, people, server), each step with what the law says, then a review. Run again at any time with "Run setup again"; "Finish community setup" is on My items until finished. | `setup_home`, `community/setup.py`, `community/us_law.py` |
| Settings | For the board, Community setup first (where the run stands and every step), then **Change one thing**: Community settings, ARC request types, Home neighbors, Letters and certificates, Paper mail settings, Common area booking rules, Branding (WT-004). Features is the setup step, Contact retention opens from the Privacy step. Other roles see the pages open to them (a sysadmin: Branding, Demo data on demo and test portals). With only one open to you, the menu and the breadcrumbs go straight to it. | `settings_hub` |
| More finance | Finance pages used now and then: Reserve plan, Online payments, Disbursements, Resale certificates (WT-036). | `finance_more` |
| Board (menu heading) | The heading over Association, Maintenance, Finance and Admin, in the board's accent colour; "Staff" for a manager, auditor, ARC reviewer or sysadmin who is not on the board (D64). | `core/navigation.py` `WORK_SECTIONS`, `work_label` |
| Board badge | The "Board" label beside the heading and the strip at the top of every page in those sections: you're acting for the association there (D64). Owners' pages never carry it, nor does a page about your own home's matter (D65). | `nav.board_page`, `base.html`, `own_matter` |
| Your own home's matter | A request, architectural request, notice, booking, account, collection case, certificate or refund about your own home (as an owner on the deed or an account contact, now or when it arose), or one you raised. Every board member can read it, but another board member takes the board's steps on it: "This is your own home's request: another board member handles it." You still act on it as the homeowner. Internal notes on it are hidden from you (D65). | `core/conflicts.py` `own_matter`, `policy.own_matter_refused` |
| Board work / Your home | The two parts of the dashboard for someone with a home and board work (D64): board work first when anything waits there. | `core.dashboard.parts`, `Item.side` |
| "… for the board" | How decision buttons read for a board member (Approve for the board, Decline for the board, Void for the board...); the confirmation ends "You're acting for the association." (D64). | `core.workflow.for_board`, `acting_confirm` |
| [Board] | The start of the subject of every board or staff notification email (D64). Personal mail never has it. | `comms.notify.board_subject` |
| Contact retention | Former owners' contact details cleared after the retention period. | `retention` |
| Server tasks | Admin page where a sysadmin starts a backup, a restore drill, the off-site lock check, re-applying host security or the health checks; the server's own agent runs them from a fixed list. The board reads the history on Sysadmin activity (WT-007). | `server_tasks`, `core/host_tasks.py`, `infra/poc/host-tasks.sh` |
| Demo data | Settings page, on demo and test portals only (404 elsewhere), where a sysadmin reseeds the portal with a fictional dataset (Oak Creek Farms full demo, or Fresh start just handed over), typing RESEED to confirm. A backup is taken first and real logins are kept; the server's agent does it as the "Reseed with demo data" server task (D53). | `demo_data`, `core/demo_reseed.py`, `community/demo/`, `infra/poc/reseed-demo.sh` |
| Access review | The board's quarterly check of every login's access, on the People page. | `access_review` |
| Homes and owners | Every home, by address, with its current owners; the "Accounts, contacts and balances" view adds each account's other people, contact details and balance, with the CSV export (was Roster); the board's "Sign-ups" view shows who has signed up, home by home, how homes get official notices, "Invite everyone not yet invited" and reminders, and every invitation with re-send and revoke (was Invitations and sign-ups, D50). | `lot_list` (`roster` redirects; `?view=signups`, `onboarding` redirects there) |
| Invitations | The sysadmin's list of invitations with re-send and revoke, under Admin. No register of owners (D9). The board's sign-ups are on **Homes and owners**. | `invitations` (the board is sent to the Sign-ups view) |
| Ask to change the owners on the deed | On My home, an owner asks the board to add an owner (joint owner, trust or entity) or remove one, with the recorded deed attached. A board member who is not the requester or the person added or removed approves; an added person with an email is invited to sign in. (Contacts: "Ask to add someone".) | `AccountChangeRequest` (`add_owner`, `remove_owner`), D41 |
| Board and committees | Who serves: board members with their offices, and each committee with its members. Names and offices only, for every resident. | `board_and_committees` |
| Common area booking rules | Each common area (name, kind, in use) and its booking rules, edited together, with where the rules come from. | `reservation_settings` |
| Recurring deadlines | The Board tasks tab for filings and renewals the association must not miss (insurance, taxes, annual report), each with a reminder window (was Compliance calendar). | `tasks?tab=deadlines`, `ComplianceItem` (`compliance` redirects) |
| Sign-in and security | Your own sign-in methods (passkey, authenticator app, recovery codes, password); also the My items group for them. Not the board's account recovery rules (Community setup step "Account recovery rules"). | `mfa_index` |
| Download my data | A file with everything the portal holds about you, for your records. | JSON export |
| RSVP | Your answer to a calendar event: Going, Maybe or Not going, with guests if the event allows. Other owners see counts only. | `comms.EventRsvp`, `comms/rsvp.py` |
| Volunteers | Jobs to sign up for at an event ("Bring a dessert"), each with a number of places. | `VolunteerSlot`, `VolunteerSignup` |
| Events I'm going to | Dashboard list of coming events you said Going to or volunteer at. | `comms.rsvp.my_upcoming` |
| Bank feed | Lines from the association's bank (through Teller, or imported statement files), matched to deposits and vendor payments for the monthly reconciliation. Board, manager and auditor only. | `ledger/bankfeeds`, `BankTransaction` |
| Connect a bank | Linking the association's bank account through Teller (board only, after a fresh password or passkey check). | `bank_connect`, Teller Connect |
| Match the columns | The step after uploading a CSV statement: which column is the date, description and amount. | `bank_import` (CSV mapping) |

Dates: "Nov 3" for a date in the current month, "Nov 3, 2027" for any other
(`{{ d|short_date }}`, `core.dates.short_date`).
