# Pages, navigation, forms, search, help and the setup wizard

Moved from CLAUDE.md when it was cut to its essentials (D90): the same rules and reference, unchanged, read when a task touches this area. A rule here binds exactly as one in CLAUDE.md, which stays the source of truth (if a task conflicts with either, stop and ask).

- New list/detail pages: register every URL in `core/tests/test_url_matrix.py` (PROTECTED with a lot-B target, or MEMBER_PAGES with a BOARD_ONLY_MARKERS entry)
- Workflows: declare transitions with `core.workflow.Workflow`; decisions that create records go through a service, not the generic action URL
- Stage tracker (D99): every workflow declares its path (`stages=Stages(...)`, or `core.workflow.track()` for a derived state machine in a `STAGE_MODULES` module) and its detail page shows `{% workflow_stages obj %}` under the heading; states only, the page's own visibility; `core/tests/test_stages.py` guards both
- Homes are named by address (D40): show `lot.label` (address) everywhere people read, `{% home_title lot %}` for a home's heading (address title, "Lot N" only in `.lot-ref`), `lot.legal_label` ("<address> (Lot N)") only on formal documents; lists in address order (`lot.sort_key`); CSV exports start with "Address" ("Lot number" last only in finance/register exports); `core/tests/test_addresses.py` checks every page
- Picking a home (D96): every "pick a home" field uses the home picker (`community.home_picker.HomeField`/`HomesField` in forms, `{% include "partials/home_picker.html" %}` in hand-written ones, resolved with `home_picker.resolve`/`resolve_many` against the form's own homes): type-ahead by street address from `home_suggest` (any signed-in member: addresses and owners' names are public, owner's decision; nothing else), a plain text box (exact address or lot number, close matches on error) without JavaScript; never a long select of homes or a lot-number box
- Merged pages (streamlining, M5): Homes and owners (`lot_list`, roster view `?view=roster`), Sign-ups (`lot_list?view=signups`, board only, `invite.send`; `invitations` redirects the board there and is the sysadmin's Invitations page, D50), Budget and reserves (`owner_budget`, in `features.ANY_OF`), Recurring deadlines (`tasks?tab=deadlines`); `roster`, `onboarding`, `reserve_summary`, `compliance` redirect there. Board and committees (`board_and_committees`, `board_committees.view`): names and offices only. View-only contacts read their account's requests; areas are edited with their booking rules; urgent texts go from the announcement form
- Lists with an owner side and a board side use `?mine=1` (`core.scoping.own_records`); the "My home" menu links there
- Menu trim (D95, 0.43.0): the owner side of Violations is **My violations**; owners' records requests are a section of My requests ("Ask for records", `SUBPAGES["requests?mine=1"]`); My reimbursements has no menu entry (Finance overview button, committee page, My items); claims are board and committee work (0.44.0)
- Portal search (`/search/`, `core/search.py`): each area registers a provider in `<app>/search.py` (listed in `PROVIDER_MODULES`) that checks its list page's permission and reuses that page's scoped queryset; thread hits go through `search.thread_results`; never log or audit the query; a new area needs a case in `core/tests/test_search.py`
- Owner help (D73, optional feature `owner_help`): How it works pages in `community/help.py` (`TOPICS`; text built from the rules at request time, with citations; a section page links its topic with `{% load help_tags %}{% how_it_works "slug" %}`; a new topic needs its builder and a search hit test), board words append-only `HelpText` rendered only by `help.render_text` (escape first, small markdown subset, never raw HTML); getting-started checklist `community/checklist.py` (steps derived from state per role, marks append-only `HelpMark` of the signed-in login only; dashboard for rule `help.welcome_days`, one My items entry for steps My items does not raise itself); the guided first sign-in ends with `help.after_setup_url` (Welcome once); "Ask the board" is `ask_board` (opens a new owner request, `?about=<type>` preselects; not behind the feature); help search `community/help_search.py`; seed `core/demo_help.py` (`seed_demo_help`)
- My items queue (`core/my_items.py`): add new "waiting on someone" work there, from scoped queries; the dashboard to-dos, the My items page (set-up warnings such as two-person coverage on top, area `Setup`; the groups as one line of jump links, each named after its menu entry, e.g. "Owner requests") and sidebar bubbles all read it (`items_for_request`, once per request); an item's `side` (`home` or `board`) decides its dashboard part
- Sidebar (`core/navigation.py`): menu label = page `<h1>` = the name in `docs/glossary.md` (a test checks); sections My home / Community / Association / Maintenance / Finance / Admin (the last four under one "Board" heading, `WORK_SECTIONS`; a former owner's one section is "My history"); for staff every section starts closed except the current page's, work sections before their own home (D100); rarely used pages go on a hub (`HUBS`: Settings, Community info, Voting) instead of the sidebar, and a hub with one page open to the user links straight to it, its breadcrumb too (`{% hub_crumb %}`); every hub tile shows its own My items count and the bubble is their sum (D89, `hub_links` `count`, `TILE_SUBPAGES`); Finance starts with the Finance overview (D89, `ledger/finance_overview.py` `CARDS`: one card per finance component with status, own count and link, grouped Money in / Money out / Books / Planning / Year end and tax, the fund-balance strip from `closed_fund_positions` on top; a new finance component adds a card (and its My items use its own page as `nav`); its bubble is the cards' sum via `AGGREGATES`, other finance pages open from it via `SUBPAGES`; `finance_more` redirects there); `TILE_GROUPS` limits a tile to some roles (Paper mail settings is the board's); the board's Settings front is Community setup with "Change one thing" tiles (`COVERED_BY_SETUP` pages are not repeated; Features is the setup step); pages reached from another page mark its entry current via `SUBPAGES`; no per-area tab bars (Finance keeps one across Finance overview, Owner accounts, Collections, Accounting, Reports). Dates in lists: `{{ d|short_date }}` (shows the year outside the current month). Workflow steps that close or refuse something carry a `confirm=` text saying what happens next
- Phone layout and UI shell (D48): fix tap targets and overflow in `static/css/site.css` at the source, not per page; tables that must read on a phone get `class="stack-sm"` with `data-label` on cells and `.cell-title` on the lead cell; a row that opens one thing is `row-link` with its link `row-main`; in-page filters are `<nav data-swap>` with links ending `#results` and the list inside `<div data-swap-region id="results">` (static/js/app.js swaps it, no HTMX); a header button for an on-page panel carries `data-opens="<panel id>"` and is rendered only while the panel is closed (one control per form); the phone tab bar comes from `core.navigation.tabbar`; error pages are `templates/403.html`/`404.html`. Automatic monthly posting's safety net (missed month: board email once a month, My items, health check "Monthly assessment posting"): `ledger/autopost.py`
- Forms: an invalid form re-renders with the bound form (never redirect), `{% include "partials/form_errors.html" %}` at the top and its panel open; service refusals go on the form with `form.add_error(None, ...)`. Several files in one upload: `documents.attachments.attach_many` (caller checks who may add). Disabled workflow steps with the reason: `Workflow.blocked()` passed as `blocked_steps`
- Owner-facing lot records (ARC, violations, resale) are limited to the owner's own era: use `core.scoping.owner_era_q` in `scope_for_owner`
- Optional features (board, Settings → Features (board)): register in `core/features.py` (`FEATURES`, with every URL name); switched-off features 404 and leave the menu
- Every new setting, optional feature or server set-up script must appear in the Community setup wizard (data-driven for rules and features; a guard test checks): a new rule group needs a step in `community/setup.py` (the catch-all is only a safety net), every `core/features.py` feature shows on the Features step with its description, and every secret-taking script in "Deployment targets" has a status line on the server step (`community/tests/test_setup_wizard_guard.py`)
- Community setup wizard (D51, Settings → Community setup, `setup_home`, board with `community.manage_rules`; manager and sysadmin no access): steps in `community/setup.py` (`STEPS`; rule steps take RuleSpecs by group and key prefix, `catch_all` takes new groups, so a new rule needs no wizard edit), pages in `community/setup_views.py` saving through the owning services (`set_rule`, `update_profile`, `update_letter_settings`, `save_common_area`, `features.set_feature`) with their confirmations; typed rule values via `community/rule_input.py` (percents and basis points typed and shown as a percent with a % after the box, `rules.value_display` everywhere a value is read; every box shows its default or a greyed "e.g." example, never the demo's "DEMO - replace"); common areas are removed there by taking them out of use (never deleted, refused while bookings are upcoming); progress append-only `SetupStepRecord` (run, step, action); "What the law says" per step from `community/pa_law.py` plus federal/other PA notes in `community/us_law.py` (each with citation and counsel caveat, or a no-specific-law note); never a field for keys
- Long pages and summaries (D100): a dashboard shows a summary of My items (counts per group linking to its card, the top few, "See all in My items"), never a copy; a long list is paged server-side (`core.lists.paginate` with `partials/pager.html`, actions on every row) or folded (`details.fold-group` with its count, `details.more-items` "Show N more"), the first screen holding what needs action; folding is markup only and never drops a record or an action; new controls are 44 px on phones
