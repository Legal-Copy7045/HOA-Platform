# Money: ledger, payments, books and finance options

Moved from CLAUDE.md when it was cut to its essentials (D90): the same rules and reference, unchanged, read when a task touches this area. A rule here binds exactly as one in CLAUDE.md, which stays the source of truth (if a task conflicts with either, stop and ask).

- Reserve plan (optional feature `reserves`, Finance → Reserve plan; owners: the Reserves section of Budget and reserves): math in `ledger/reserves.py` (pure, integer cents, rounding in its docstring), services in `ledger/reserve_plan.py`; "Record as adopted" writes append-only `ReserveStudySnapshot`, cited on the budget page and offered on Letters
- Bank feed (optional feature `bank_feeds`, D32-bankfeeds): `ledger/bankfeeds/` (providers `teller` (stdlib mTLS client, `api.teller.io` only) and `file` (OFX/QFX parser, CSV mapping); append-only `BankTransaction`; `matching.py` suggests, people confirm, reconcile pre-ticks; tokens Fernet-encrypted under `BANK_FEED_TOKEN_KEY`; Teller CSP only on `bank_connect`, scripts from `cdn.teller.io/connect/` only, unpinned by necessity, D34-fixinfra-2); descriptions always pass `base.clean_description` (long digit runs masked to the last four, D34-fixinfra-1); one-time set-up on the VM with `sudo bash infra/poc/set-teller.sh` (checks with `manage.py check_teller`; `docs/runbook.md` "Bank feed")
- Open charges and online payments (D32-payments): `ledger/allocation.py` (append-only `PaymentApplication`, `open_charges`/`charge_status`/`apply`; every new ledger entry is applied by a post_save hook); Stripe in `payments/` (`checkout.py` start and verify, `sync.py` polling, payouts, `payouts_for_matching`, reconciliation; `stripe_api.py` urllib client, tests use `payments/tests/fake_stripe.py`); key on the VM with `sudo bash infra/poc/set-stripe-key.sh` (checks it with `manage.py check_stripe`); never `method="online"` by hand
- Streamlined money and ARC steps: delinquency letters only from the collection case (`collection_letter`, reprints `collection_notice_pdf`; the Home account page has "Open collection case", `lot_notice_pdf` redirects); balance to-do "Pay $X" → `pay_online?all=1` (methods first, `?choose=1` picks charges; the server always recomputes); a to-do's shortcut button is `Item.action_label`/`action_url`; staff shortcuts `core.my_items.quick_actions` / `{% quick_actions %}`; owner ARC tabs Mine / Asked of me (`arc/_owner_tabs.html`)
- Ledger money per ownership account (D33-acctfix): `ledger/accounts.py` (entries carry their `Account` from the posting date; money applies only within an account; readers take `account=ALL|CURRENT|id`; owner pages, letters and resale use `CURRENT`); refunds at Stripe: `payments.services.refund_portions` / `record_refund`; ownership date changes move entries by reversal + copy (`ledger.services.move_entries`, `account_periods_change` locks the lot), each move leaves an `EntryMoveNotice` My item for the board. The board moves a payment or credit to another account of the lot with Move to account on the Home account page (`services.reassign_entry`, `ledger.reassign`, board only, reason required, reversal + copy, audited `ledger.entry_reassigned`); a later date change leaves it there. Account period changes (D34-fixmoney) go through `ledger.services.account_periods_change`, which moves entries to the account they now belong to (reversal + copy with `moved_from`; owner pages exclude `accounts.MOVED_AWAY`); late fees follow their dues, online payments the checkout's account
- The monthly charge's name (D42): never write "dues" where people read it; use `community.terms` (`charge_term()`, `term_text("... {term} ...")`) or `{% term "Terms" %}` / `{% with_term "..." %}` (rule `charge.term`, default Assessment; a test guards templates). New monthly charges are "<Term> – <Month YYYY>"
- Monthly statement email (D42): `ledger/statement_email.py`, queued by `post_monthly_assessments` after commit (`ledger.tasks.send_statement_emails`); on by default (`ContactProfile.statement_emails`, untick on My profile); owners and authorized/view-only contacts only; once per account and month (append-only `StatementEmail`); current account's PDF attached; autopay line via `payments.autopay.describe_for_statement` when present
- Automatic payments (optional feature `autopay`, D46): `payments/autopay.py` (setup-mode Checkout bound to `AutopaySetup`, one Stripe Customer per account, append-only `AutopayEvent` state and `AutopayAttempt` per account-month-try, hourly task `payments.tasks.autopay`; charges only the month's assessment on the due day; `describe_for_statement(account)` for statement emails); the account-period hook `payments.services.ownership_changed` cancels it on a sale. Schedule (D47): monthly or once a year (`AutopaySchedule`, set on the set-up or changed on the Autopay page via `change_schedule`, audited; annual charges the next 12 months less credit on the chosen month's due day, attempt period "YYYY", key `autopay-<acct>-annual-<year>-<try>`, money drawn down by open-item allocation like any prepayment; attempts record `covers_from`/`covers_through`; months no yearly payment holds are "bridged" by the monthly path (`covered_by_annual`, `annual_window`), so no month is uncovered or charged twice). Review (D47): any automatic charge above rule `payments.autopay_review_cents` (default $500, surcharge included) is held as an append-only `AutopayReview` (`payments/autopay_review.py`); a board member (`payments.manage`, never the manager, never someone who pays for that home) approves (charged on the next run) or declines with a reason (payers emailed); expires after 14 days; board My item "Approve automatic payment of $X for <address>"; `payments.autopay_ceiling_percent` is retired (`community.rules.RETIRED`)
- Disbursements (D52, optional feature `disbursements`, Finance → Disbursements): `ledger/disbursements.py` (surplus distributions (D67): split by rule `disbursement.surplus_by_share_paid` (default: in proportion to the fiscal year's assessments paid, `assessments_paid`; off: equal per home), credited to the home's account today (`disbursement.surplus_stays_with_home`, default) or to the ownership account that paid (a seller's old account), statement credits only unless `disbursement.surplus_cash_allowed`; largest remainder in address order, resolution required; wizard step "Surplus and refunds"; owner refunds ≤ the account's credit, checked at propose, approve and issue); append-only batches, lines, decisions, checks and check events; manager proposes, a different board member approves (two at `disbursement.second_approval_cents`; refund recipients never; surplus recipients may, then two needed); statement credits post on approval, refund checks post a `refund` entry when issued, surplus checks post no owner entry; void reverses (board only); checks only from the check register or pre-printed stock (`core.pdf.render_positioned`), never bank numbers; journal account `surplus_fund`; outstanding checks in reconciliation and bank-feed matching (kind `check`)
- Journal kinds (0.40.0): `interest` and `legal_cost` post to their own income accounts (`ledger.models.OWNER_FINANCE_GL`, operating), `special_assess` to special assessment income in the levy's fund (interfund lines); all three are exempt-function (member) income on the 1120-H worksheet, bank interest is not. Taxes read vendor onboarding (`tax.w9_on_file` → `vendor_onboarding.w9_on_file`, `tax.needs_1099` → `eligible_for_1099`, unknown = yes); renewals read `vendor_onboarding.coi_expires_on` (the later of onboarding's and the vendor record's date). Vendor checks are seen by the books as `InvoicePayment`s (no separate outstanding-check list) and are never offered for an unapproved vendor
- Books and funds (D69, feature `books`, on): `ledger/books/` (models in `ledger/books/models.py`, imported by `ledger/models.py`); every bank account (`BankConnection`) in the books has a fund (operating/reserve) and opening balance (`BankAccountSetup`, Finance overview → Bank accounts and funds; one primary operating account holds unmatched deposits, payouts, vendor payments and checks); money outside owner accounts and invoices enters by coding bank lines to `GLCategory` (append-only `GLEntry` with each side's fund, coded by a rule id or a person; undo = reversal); board `CodingRule`s run after every import/sync and before each close (`coding.apply_rules`, suggestions after `books.rule_suggest_after`); unambiguous matches confirmed as SYSTEM (`coding.auto_match`, `books.auto_match`); the journal (`accounting.journal_lines`, structured `journal.JLine`) carries funds and adds "3950" interfund lines so each fund balances; manager codes and prepares, the board decides
- Month-end close (D70): `ledger/books/close.py` (Finance → Month and year close, `books_close`; daily task `ledger.tasks.books_auto_close` from day `books.close_day`, closes as SYSTEM when every blocking check passes and `books.auto_close`; append-only `CloseRun`, `PeriodClose`, `ExceptionNote` + `ExceptionAgreement` (a money difference or a manager's note needs a different board member), `PeriodReopenRequest` + `PeriodReopenDecision` (two board members)); per-account reconciliation in `journal.reconcile_account`; the lock: services call `ledger.books.periods.refuse_closed`, automatic corrections use `periods.open_date` (today, with a reference), and trigger `ledger_books_refuse_closed` refuses rows dated in a closed month on ledger entries, GL entries, deposits, owner checks and vendor payments; My items via `close.board_items`
- Year end, statements and package (D71, feature `financial_statements`): `ledger/books/statements.py` (balance sheet by fund, income/expenses, budget vs actual, reserve activity, aging, `annual_accounting`; PDF/CSV audited), `ledger/books/package.py` (append-only `YearEndPackage` stored under a random key, streamed after `books.package` (board, auditor), manifest.json with SHA-256 per file and HMAC-SHA256 keyed by `BOOKS_PACKAGE_KEY` else derived from the secret, `manage.py verify_books_package`; treasurer handover sheet), optional QuickBooks Online layout `ledger/books/quickbooks.py`; tax (D71-tax) `ledger/books/tax.py` (Form 1120-H worksheet and 60%/90% tests, rate `tax.hoa_rate_bp`; 1099 totals, IRIS CSV and recipient copies with NO TINs ever stored; `w9_on_file`/`needs_1099` the one adapter for vendor onboarding; filing deadlines on Recurring deadlines; nothing filed); demo data `ledger/demo_books.py` (`seed_demo_books`)
- Finance options (D80-D85), each an optional feature, all off by default, rules on the setup step "Interest, forecast, legal, projects and claims"; the sources in other areas are one function each, wired at 0.40.0 (`ledger/tests/test_release_040_wiring.py`): `cashflow.opening_balances` and `owner_finance.fund_balances` read the books' funds at the last closed month (`ledger.books.close.closed_fund_positions`) and `owner_finance.budget_vs_actual_ytd` the books' statements for closed months, else today's figures marked not closed; `interest.on_payment_plan` → `ledger.payment_plans.plan_kept`, `interest.principal_kinds` includes `special_assess`; `cashflow.special_installments` → `ledger.special_assessments.due_by_month` by fund (operating ones add to collections); `capital_projects` links a `SpecialAssessment` (optional FK, typed reference as fallback); `insurance_claims.policy_for` reads the renewals `InsurancePolicy` matching the typed number or carrier (typed text the fallback): interest on overdue balances (`overdue_interest`, D80, `ledger/interest.py`: only with `interest.annual_rate_bp` cited; simple, monthly on `interest.posting_day`, one append-only `interest` entry per account and month (constraint), principal = assessments past `interest.grace_days`, fees and earlier interest only with `interest.include_fees`, none during a payment plan; the calculation is the statement line; board posts by hand (own home skipped) and reverses with a reason; daily task `ledger.tasks.overdue_interest`; policy line on My account); cash-flow forecast (`cashflow_forecast`, D81, `ledger/cashflow.py`: pure `project()`, `build()` from the cash book, assessments × collection rate, invoices, contracts and the reserve plan; warnings below `cashflow.min_operating_cents` or the plan's path; what-ifs never saved; CSV/PDF audited); liens and legal collection on a referred collection case (`collections_legal`, D82, `ledger/legal.py`, append-only `LegalEvent`, board only, legal panel on the case page, costs charged as `legal_cost` only by a board member with `legal.costs_chargeable` cited, never one's own home; My item to file the release when paid); capital projects (`capital_projects`, D83, `operations/capital_projects.py`: approvals and change orders two at `capital.second_approval_cents`, append-only links, spent = payments on linked invoices, owners' Community projects summary); owners' finance summary (`owner_finance_dashboard`, D84, `ledger/owner_finance.py`: Budget and reserves shows only operating vs reserve and budget vs actual); insurance claims (`insurance_claims`, D85, `operations/insurance_claims.py`: board and manager record, board accepts settlements, two at `insurance.second_approval_cents`, deadlines on My items). Models in `ledger/models_options.py` and `operations/models_options.py`; demo data `ledger/demo_finance_options.py`
- Special assessments (D75, optional feature `special_assessments`, Finance overview): `ledger/special_assessments.py` (board levies under a resolution, owners' vote above rule `special_assessment.vote_required_cents` linked by reference; equal shares by rule `special_assessment.equal_shares` or a custom table; installments post as entry kind `special_assess` "<Title> – installment k of N" on their due dates by the daily task `ledger.tasks.special_assessments`, once per home (`SpecialAssessmentCharge`); late fees only when levied with `special_assessment.late_fees`; owner notice via `send_now`; future installments cancelled with a reason; `SpecialAssessment.fund` operating/reserve for the general ledger); owners see them on My account
- Payment plans (D76, optional feature `payment_plans`): `ledger/payment_plans.py` (prepared from the collection case by board or manager for the current account's open charges; approved by another board member, which applies the collection step "Payment plan agreed" (guarded: only with an approved plan); met/missed from allocation; daily `ledger.tasks.payment_plans` completes or defaults as SYSTEM through `plan_end_*` back to the prior state; late fees suspended while current by rule `payment_plan.suspend_late_fees`; owner agrees on My account; letter `ledger.letters.payment_plan_pdf`)
- Expense reimbursements and vendor checks (D77, features `reimbursements`, `vendor_checks`): `operations/reimbursements.py` (claim with receipt through the attachments scan path; approved by board members other than the claimant, two at `reimbursement.second_approval_cents`, only with a clean receipt; becomes an approved `Invoice` from the payee "Expense reimbursements" so `InvoicePayment` books it by category; never on the 1099 worksheet; D66 for paying), `operations/vendor_checks.py` (check stock printing with the disbursement `CheckPrintSettings`, issue records the `InvoicePayment`, void (board) a negative payment and invoice step `payment_voided`, cleared, `outstanding_checks(as_of)`); check numbers unique across all association checks via `ledger/check_numbers.py`
- Budget preparation (D78): `ledger/budget_prep.py` (Budgets → Start next year's budget: from this year's budget or actuals, increase in basis points, adopted reserve plan contribution, monthly charge per home rounded up by rule `budget.assessment_rounding_cents`; Budget compared page `budget_compare`); notices and adoption stay on the budget page
- Money tools: `ledger/accounting.py` (journal CSV export, deposit batches, bank reconciliation, 1099 worksheet, courtesy reminders, collections letter sequence); letters and resale certificate wording: `ledger/letters.py`, Finance → Letters. The statement (D40, `letters.statement_paragraphs`, also attached to notices) carries a "Pay online" QR code only when online payments are usable: a plain signed-in address (`payments.services.statement_pay_url` → `pay_start`, which opens the reader's own home), encoded by `core/qr.py` (segno) and drawn by `core.pdf.QrBlock` (booleans only)
- Vendor onboarding (D79, optional feature `vendor_onboarding`, Finance → Vendors → Vendor onboarding): `operations/vendor_onboarding.py` (services and the stable functions others call: `w9_on_file`, `w9_received_on`, `coi_current`, `required_documents`, `is_active`, `eligible_for_1099`, `invoice_block_reason`, `person_conflicted`), models `operations/onboarding_models.py`, views `operations/onboarding_views.py`. The board or manager invites (vendor created inactive); the worker mints the link token (`operations.tasks.send_vendor_onboarding_link`); the vendor's answers lock into an append-only `OnboardingSubmission`; two different board members approve (never the inviter, never a Person linked to the vendor's conflict disclosure, `VendorConflict`, which is also `Invoice.conflict_of_interest` for D65); return issues a new link, decline closes. Invoices of an unapproved or inactive vendor are neither approved, pre-approved by contract nor paid (`operations.services._vendor_may_be_paid`); vendors older than the feature are grandfathered active with a "Complete onboarding" My item. Required documents per kind of work are data with citations (`DEFAULT_REQUIREMENTS`, board changes as append-only `VendorRequirement` on Vendor requirements and terms; W-9 forced when 1099-eligible by `ENTITY_1099`, preparer caveat); terms are versioned `VendorTerms`. Uploads PDF only, size limit before buffering, scan gate, encrypted with AES-256-GCM under `VENDOR_DOCUMENT_KEY` (`operations/vendor_crypto.py`, fallbacks `VENDOR_DOCUMENT_KEY_FALLBACKS`; set on the VM with `sudo bash infra/poc/set-vendor-key.sh [--rotate]`, checked by `manage.py check_vendor_key [--reencrypt]`; no key: no uploads or viewing). No TIN or bank number is ever a field (free text refusing TIN-like numbers); W-9s open only for board members (`vendor.w9_view`, never the manager), audited and listed as the vendor's document access history; W-9 retention rule `vendor.w9_retention_years` then two-person disposal (`vendor_w9_retention`). Key kept apart from backups (backups hold ciphertext only)
- Renewals (D72, optional feature `renewals`, Maintenance → Renewals): `documents/renewals.py` (association `InsurancePolicy` terms, next term `renews` the last and versions its document; append-only `VendorCertificate` sets the vendor's expiry via `operations.services.save_vendor`, read only through `vendor_coi_expiry(vendor)`; `ContractTerms` beside the frozen contract: auto/manual, notice days, renewal months; deadline = end less notice for auto, else end; rules `renewals.*_days` with citations; My items area "Renewals" (board, manager); one "[Board]" email per item and threshold (rule days, 30, 7, 0; append-only `RenewalReminder`); board-only append-only `RenewalDecision` per contract term; `overdue_count()` for a health line; resale paragraph 9 reads `resale_insurance(profile)`). Records retention (D72, feature `records_retention`, Settings → Records retention): `documents/retention.py` (rules `records.keep_years.<category>`, basis in `community.rules.RECORDS_SCHEDULE`: document date `Document.dated_on`, superseded, contract end; nightly `records_retention_scan` lists only; disposal = proposal + a different board member's confirmation, append-only `DocumentDisposal`, stored file deleted, version row kept, `services.downloadable` refuses it; `LegalHold` on a document or category blocks every step). Document Access history (`document_access`, board and auditor, audit events, no IPs) and Compare versions (`document_compare`, txt/csv only)
