# Authorization, own matters and roles

Moved from CLAUDE.md when it was cut to its essentials (D90): the same rules and reference, unchanged, read when a task touches this area. A rule here binds exactly as one in CLAUDE.md, which stays the source of truth (if a task conflicts with either, stop and ask).

## Authorization (critical)

- A `manager` role (management-company staff) does data entry and workflow steps; it never approves invoices, decides ARC or violations, changes rules, or votes.
- Owner access derives from Ownership. Board membership derives from an active `BoardSeat` (declarant_appointed or owner_elected, with term dates; ends only when `ended_on` is recorded). Offices (president, vice_president, treasurer, secretary) are separate `OfficeHolding` records and require an active seat. Other roles (arc_reviewer, auditor, sysadmin) are dated `RoleAssignment` records; ARC appointments record who appointed them.
- `Person` (community identity) is separate from `User` (login). Ownerships, seats, offices and roles attach to Person.
- Permissions live in one map in `core/policy.py`. For now every board member can perform every board action. Every action must remain performable by at least two people with MFA; the app warns when it is not.
- No self-grants. Granting sysadmin needs a nominator and a different approving board member; revoking needs one. Invitations grant nothing: they only link a login to an existing Person.
- Board seats (D66): the start-up exemption from the second approval is one-time (only before a second seat has ever been recorded; ended seats never reopen it); after that a seat needs a second board member, except winners of a certified owners' election while fewer than two seats are active. A board member selling their home resigns first (My items reminds the board).
- Payments to board members (D66, `ledger.disbursements.payment_refusal`): nobody takes a step on a payment to an account they are or were ever on the deed of, and a check to a board member's account is printed, written, voided or cleared only by another board member, never the manager; refund checks are made out to today's owners (open account) or the owners at the sale (`payee_owners`), never a removed co-owner.
- Owner access derives from an active `Ownership` record (person to lot, with start and end dates). Ending an ownership removes access automatically; history stays on the lot.
- Board members are homeowners first: their ownership is recorded like anyone's (register import, or by another board member), and board rights from the seat stack on top. One login and one menu (D64: no workspace switch, and no extra sign-in to open a board page): "My home" and "Community", then the work sections (Association, Maintenance, Finance, Admin) under one "Board" heading in the accent colour; ending a seat removes only the board side. Board cues (D64): a work-section page gets the "Board" badge and strip from `nav.board_page` (never per template; "Staff" for staff not on the board); decision buttons use `for_board` / `acting_confirm` (`core.workflow`, `{% for_board %}`, `{% acting_confirm %}`, `partials/workflow.html` for `DECISION_ACTIONS`), and a decision a service refuses as one's own (own home, own request, own refund) says so before any button (`{% own_home_note %}`); the dashboard of someone with both shows "Board work" and "Your home" (`Item.side`, `core.dashboard.parts`); board and staff notification emails go through `comms.notify` with `board=True` / `mail_board` ("[Board] " subject), personal mail never does.
- Accounts (Milestone 3): an `Account` is one ownership period of a lot (numbered; dormant once the lot is sold). People not on the deed attach as `AccountContact` with a kind: authorized (sees the account, raises requests), view_only (sees, cannot act), tenant (community pages and own requests, no balance or votes). Contacts are added only by the board or manager.
- Owner requests (D41, My home, `community.services.request_account_change` / `decide_account_change`): owners on the deed of the active account ask; a board member other than the requester approves. Adding a contact or owner needs their email unless "They don't use email" (`no_login`, record only, no sign-in); approval then invites them as the approving board member (to the Person's email; a failed invitation leaves the approval and tells the board to use the Sign-ups view of Homes and owners). Deed changes (`add_owner` joint/trust/entity, `remove_owner`) need the deed date and the recorded deed (attachment, served by `account_change_deed` to the board and that account's owners only); the approver is never the person added or removed, the account keeps at least one owner (a sale is a board transfer), a deed dated before the account began is recorded by the board from the home's page; sole/joint types follow through `set_ownership_type`.
- Former owners keep a read-only login that shows only their own records from their ownership period (`/my-history/`, `history.view_own`, which also lists their old requests); services refuse any change from them (`community.services._refuse_read_only`). Nothing after the sale, and no current community pages.
- Every model that belongs to a lot or person must be reachable only through a scoped manager/queryset (e.g. `Model.objects.for_user(user)`). The default manager must not return unscoped data to owner-facing views. Views must never fetch by bare primary key without scoping.
- Pattern (`core/scoping.py`): subclass `ScopedModel`, set `scope_lot_path` or `scope_person_path`, use a `ScopedQuerySet` manager. Unscoped queries raise `UnscopedQueryError`; services use `.unscoped(reason)` or `system_access(reason)`. Views use `PolicyRequiredMixin` / `ScopedObjectMixin` (`core/mixins.py`); out-of-scope objects are 404.
- Services take an explicit `actor` and call `core.policy.require()` themselves. Trusted internal code (bootstrap, seed data) passes `core.policy.SYSTEM`; views always pass `request.user`.
- Every feature ships with cross-owner access tests: owner A must get 403/404 for owner B's lot, documents, requests, and files, on every URL and download path.
- ARC recusal: a reviewer who is the applicant, or whose lot is affected, cannot view deliberations or vote on that request. Enforced in the service layer and tested.
- Own matters (D65, `core/conflicts.py`): board members' requests are homeowners' requests; every board member may read all of them, their own included, but nobody advances their own matter with board or staff powers. A matter is one's own when one is its requester, applicant or subject, an owner or account contact of its home today, or owned the home when it arose (models name their parties with `scope_lot_path` / `scope_person_path`, `conflict_user_fields`, `conflict_person_fields`, or `conflict_of_interest(user)`, e.g. ARC recusal). Services refuse with `conflicts.refuse(actor, obj, area=..., error=...)` before their transaction (audited `policy.own_matter_refused`, ids only); `core.workflow` refuses every staff-only step (`conflicts.staff_permission`) itself and shows it disabled via `Workflow.blocked()`. Covered: owner requests (steps, assign, work order, staff photos), ARC (all staff steps, positions, affected homes, decisions; recusal now includes account contacts and the home's era), violations (steps, follow-up, evidence photos), records requests, resale certificates, bookings, account changes and the people and owners on the account, collection cases (steps, letters, notices, paper copies, follow-up), ledger entries (charges, credits, payments by hand, reversals, moves, move checks), online-payment problems and refunds, automatic payment reviews, refund disbursements. The owner side stays (reply as the requester, withdraw, respond, add owner photos, cancel a booking, pay). Detail pages pass `conflicts.page_context(user, obj, workflow)`: `own_matter` drops the Board badge and strip (`base.html`), `own_note` replaces the staff controls. Internal thread notes on one's own matter are hidden (`comms.threads._recused`). Staff My items leave out one's own matters; `conflicts.coverage_gaps` puts "Only you can act on … and it's your own — another board member is needed" on My items when no other person with two-step sign-in can act.
- Sysadmin actions are audited and visible to the Board. Sysadmins get operations pages only, no owner data by virtue of the role. No Django admin in production. Break-glass management commands require `--actor` and `--reason` and are audited.
