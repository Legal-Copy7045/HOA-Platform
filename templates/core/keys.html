Keys and connections

The keys and settings of the services the portal uses: email, payments, the bank feed, USPS tracking, the bounce mailbox, reading signed forms and the vendor document key. Each is tested before it is saved, stored encrypted and never shown again: only Set or Not set, the last four characters, who saved it and when (decision D93). Changes take effect within a few seconds, without a restart.
