Recover your sign-in

For homeowners, account contacts and tenants who lost their phone or passkey and their recovery codes.

  1. Enter your email. If your account can be recovered this way, we email you a link (it works once, for an hour).
  2. Open it and confirm. Your recovery then waits {{ wait }} hours. You, every email address we have for you, your phone (if you get our texts) and the board are told at once, and anyone can cancel it.
  3. After the wait, ask here for a new link and open it on the phone or computer you'll use. Your old passkeys, app and codes are removed and you set up a new one.
  4. For {{ lock }} hours afterwards you can't vote, give a proxy, change your contact details or notice choices, or set up automatic payments.

Board members, officers and the association's staff can't use this: two board members reset your sign-in instead.
