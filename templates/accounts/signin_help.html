Sign-in help

I forgot my password

We can email you a link to choose a new one. You'll still need your passkey or authenticator app afterwards.

{% icon "mail" %} Email me a password reset link

Have a passkey? You don't need your password at all: use "Sign in with a passkey".

I lost my phone or passkey

Sign in with your password as usual. When asked for the 6-digit code, type one of your recovery codes instead. Then add a new passkey or app under "Sign-in and security".

If you have a passkey on another device, you can use that instead.

I have no recovery codes either

The board can reset your sign-in. For your safety this takes two board members: one checks it's really you and starts the reset, and a second approves it. The second one then gives you a one-time code in person or by phone (never by email).

Next, sign in with your password, type that code, and add a new passkey or authenticator app within {{ window }}, in the same browser.
