Sign-in help

I forgot my password

We can email you a link to choose a new one. Before you choose it you'll still need your passkey, your authenticator app or one of your recovery codes.

{% icon "mail" %} Email me a password reset link

Have a passkey? You don't need your password at all: use "Sign in with a passkey".

I lost my phone or passkey

Sign in with your password as usual. When asked for the 6-digit code, type one of your recovery codes instead. If you have a passkey on another device (your computer, a second phone) or an authenticator app, use that. Then add a new passkey or app under "Sign-in and security".

I've lost my phone and recovery codes

Homeowners, account contacts and tenants can recover their sign-in themselves. For your safety it waits {{ recovery_wait }} hours: you, every email address we have for you, your phone (if you get our texts) and the board are told at once, and anyone can cancel. Afterwards you set up a new passkey or app, and for {{ recovery_lock }} hours voting, proxies, contact details and automatic payments are locked.

{% icon "key" %} I've lost my phone and recovery codes

Board members, officers and staff

The board resets your sign-in instead. For your safety this takes two board members: one checks it's really you and starts the reset, and a second approves it. The second one then gives you a one-time code in person or by phone (never by email). Homeowners can ask for this too.

Next, sign in with your password, type that code, and add a new passkey or authenticator app within {{ window }}, in the same browser.

{% icon "info" %}{% if contact %}To start a reset, contact {{ contact }}.{% else %}To start a reset, contact a board member.{% endif %}

To avoid all this: add a second passkey or an authenticator app as a backup, and keep your recovery codes printed with your important papers.
