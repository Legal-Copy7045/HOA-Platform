Bank categories and rules

Money that passes through no owner account and no invoice (bank interest, bank fees, insurance or utilities paid by bank debit, transfers between the association's accounts) enters the books when its bank line is coded to a category. Rules code new lines by themselves; deposits, payouts and checks are matched on the Bank feed instead.
