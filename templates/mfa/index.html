What's this?

A passkey is a secure key your phone or computer keeps for this portal. You unlock it the way you unlock your device: fingerprint, face or PIN. It can't be guessed or phished, and there's nothing to remember.

An authenticator app (for example Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or your password manager) shows a short code you type after your password.

An emailed sign-in code never counts: it only replaces the password, and you still need your passkey or app.
