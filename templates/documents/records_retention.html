Records retention

How long each kind of document is kept. Nothing is removed by itself: files past their period are listed here every night, a board member proposes disposal, and a different board member confirms it. Disposal removes the stored file only; the record of the document, every version's name, size and checksum, and the disposal itself (what, when, who and why) are kept. A legal hold stops disposal. Backups made earlier still hold the file until they age out.
