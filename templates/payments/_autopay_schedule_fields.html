How often

Once a year: until your first yearly payment, each month is paid automatically as usual. The yearly payment becomes credit on your account, and each month's {% term "term" %} is paid from it as it is charged. If the {% term "term" %} changes during the year, the credit simply runs out earlier or later; anything left after a sale stays with your account. If you change the month, the next yearly payment pays for the 12 months after your prepaid months end, and any month in between is paid automatically as usual; no month is charged twice. Credit pays any other open charge first, in the association's usual order. Payments above {{ review_cents|cents }} wait for the board's review before they are charged.
